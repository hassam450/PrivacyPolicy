# Floor Plan Maps Privacy Policy

**App:** Floor Plan Maps: House Design (Android, package `com.tradewyn.floorplan`)
**Developer:** Tradewyn
**Effective date:** November 16, 2026

## Summary

Floor Plan Maps shows house plans, design ideas and building cost calculators. All plans and designs are built into the app, and everything you do in it stays on your phone. The developer has no servers and never receives your data. This version of the app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account or sign-in.

## Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, send or share any personal information, including your name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. It does not use the advertising ID.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Internet:** the app lists this permission, but it does not use the internet to send or receive your data. All plans, designs and calculators work offline.

The app does not ask for camera, storage, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

## Photos you choose

Some screens let you pick a photo from your gallery or take one with your camera:

- Gallery photos are picked with Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose, not your other photos.
- Camera photos are taken with your phone's own camera app, and only the photo you take is returned to Floor Plan Maps.
- The photo is used only on your phone, inside the app. It is never uploaded. A temporary copy is kept in the app's cache, which Android can clear at any time and which is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Sketches and saved designs

- **My Creation:** sketches you save in the sketch editor are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete them in My Creation, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.
- **Download:** when you download a plan or design, it is saved to your phone's Pictures folder (in "House Designs"), or to a place you choose. Those copies stay in your gallery after you uninstall the app, and you can delete them from your gallery.

## Calculators

The numbers you type into the bricks, steel, paint and tiles calculators are used only to show the result on your phone. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

## Settings stored on your phone

The app stores a small setting on your phone, such as whether you have already seen the welcome screens. It contains no personal information and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's settings and saved sketches in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings. The developer cannot access it.

## Sharing and links

- When you share a plan or design, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
- "Rate us", "More apps" and similar links open Google Play or your browser. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Children's privacy

The app is meant for adults planning or building a home and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Because the app does not collect or send personal data, there is no personal information held by the developer that could be exposed.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding ads or analytics, this page and the effective date above will be updated before that version is released.
