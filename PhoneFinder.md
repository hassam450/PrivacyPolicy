# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26-11-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Find My Phone** (also listed as *Find Phone By Clap*, the "App"), published by **Tradewyn** ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App. The App is distributed on Google Play under the package name `com.tradewyn.phonefinder`.

## Summary

- **Your microphone audio never leaves your phone.** Clap and whistle detection runs entirely on your device. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- The free App **shows ads** served by **Google AdMob**.
- The App uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to count anonymous app events, such as turning the finder on or finding your phone.
- Optional **Pro** purchases are handled by **Google Play** and managed by **RevenueCat**.
- The App has **no account** and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location or files.
- We do not run our own servers and do not sell your personal information.

## 1. Microphone (audio)

The App asks for the **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)** permission so it can hear a clap or whistle. Audio is:

- analysed in real time, **entirely on your device**;
- **never recorded** to a file, **never stored**, and **never transmitted** to us, to Google, to RevenueCat or to anyone else;
- never used for ads or analytics. No sound, sound level or audio-derived data is included in any analytics event.

The microphone is only used while the finder is switched on. A persistent notification is shown the whole time, as Android requires, and listening stops when you switch the finder off.

## 2. Motion, battery and charger alarms

Optional security alarms (motion, pocket, touch, charger unplug and battery alarms) read the phone's motion sensors and battery/charger state. This is processed **only on your device** to decide whether to sound an alarm and is never sent anywhere.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free App shows ads from **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

- your device's **advertising ID** and app set ID;
- **approximate location**, inferred from your IP address;
- **app interactions** (for example, ads viewed or tapped) and **diagnostic** information;

and may use them for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The App declares the `AD_ID` permission for this purpose.

**Your choices**

- If you are in the **European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland**, you are asked for consent (through Google's consent form, using the IAB TCF framework) before any personalised ad is requested. Refusing consent keeps the App fully working. You can change your choice at any time from **Menu → Privacy Policy & Safety → Privacy choices**.
- On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- Buying Pro removes ads.

Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## 4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The App uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the App and its advertising perform. It records anonymous app events such as turning the finder on, finding your phone, running the in-app sound test, opening the Pro screen, purchases and ads shown, together with device information (for example, device model, Android version and app version) and an app instance identifier.

- Analytics **never receives audio** or anything derived from it.
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics collection follows the consent choice you make in Google's consent form and is off until you give that consent.
- The data is used only to improve the App and measure its advertising. It is not used to identify you.

Firebase privacy information: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## 5. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Pro is sold through **Google Play** as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. **We never see your name, email address or payment details.**

To unlock and restore Pro, the App uses **RevenueCat, Inc.**, which receives an **anonymous app user ID** generated by the App, your **purchase history** for this App, and basic device information. RevenueCat acts as our service provider.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>

You can also unlock Pro features for a limited time by watching a rewarded ad. That ad is served by AdMob as described in section 3.

## 6. Information stored on your device

Your settings (for example, whether the finder is on, your ringtone, flash mode, sensitivity and Pro status) are stored locally on your device with Android's SharedPreferences. They are removed when you uninstall the App. If you choose your own ringtone, the App keeps only a reference to that file on your device. The file is never uploaded.

## 7. Permissions the App uses

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) | Hear a clap or whistle, on device only |
| Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | Show the "finder is on" notification and important notices |
| Foreground service (microphone) | Keep listening while the App is in the background |
| Vibration, wake lock | Vibrate and keep the alarm running while it sounds |
| Run at startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Remind you to switch the finder back on after a restart |
| Ignore battery optimisations | Let you stop the phone from closing the finder in the background |
| Internet, network state | Ads, analytics and purchases |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Ads and ad measurement (section 3) |
| Billing | Pro purchases through Google Play |

The flashlight is controlled without camera access. The App does **not** request camera, location, contacts, storage, phone or SMS permissions.

## 8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play and RevenueCat), for the purposes described. All network traffic from the App is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## 9. Data retention and deletion

We do not run servers and do not hold a copy of your data. Data collected by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their own retention policies, linked above. You can:

- reset your advertising ID, as described in section 3;
- clear the App's data or uninstall the App, which removes everything stored on your device and starts a new anonymous identifier;
- email us (below) to ask for any data linked to your App's identifiers to be deleted. We will forward the request to the relevant provider where we can.

## 10. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is **not directed at children under 13** (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or under Indian, Californian or other privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing. Because the App has no account, most of these choices are available directly through the in-app privacy choices and your device's ad settings. For anything else, contact us.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. Changes will be posted on this page and the "Effective date" above will be updated.
