# ScanBee Privacy Policy

  **App:** ScanBee: QR & Barcode Scanner (Android, package `com.tradewyn.qr`)
  **Developer:** Hassam Ashraf
  **Effective date:** November 4, 2026

  ## Summary

  ScanBee scans and creates QR codes and barcodes on your phone. Your scans, the codes you create, and your camera images
  stay on your device. The developer has no servers and never receives them. This version of the app has no ads, no analytics
  run by the developer, and no account or sign-in.

  ## Permissions the app uses

  - **Camera:** to read codes while the scan screen is open. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not saved or
  sent anywhere by the app.
  - **Storage (Android 9 and older only):** to save a QR code you created as an image in your Pictures folder, only when you
  tap Save. Newer Android versions don't need this permission.
  - **Vibration:** to buzz when a code is found. You can turn this off in Settings.
  - **Internet and network state:** these are added by Google's ML Kit library, which the app uses to read barcodes. See
  "Google ML Kit" below. The app itself doesn't use the internet to send your data.

  ## Photos you choose

  If you scan from your gallery or share an image to ScanBee, only that image is read, on your device, to find a code. The
  app doesn't get access to your other photos and doesn't keep a copy of the image.

  ## Scan history

  Scanned content is saved on your device so you can see it in History. It can include sensitive content such as Wi-Fi
  passwords or contact details. History:

  - is never sent to the developer or anyone else;
  - is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers;
  - can be turned off, and items or all of it can be deleted, in the app;
  - is deleted when you uninstall the app.

  ## Actions you choose

  Depending on what a code contains, you can open a link, connect to a Wi-Fi network, add a contact or event, call, send a
  message or email, or copy text to your clipboard. These happen only when you tap them, and they hand the content to Android
  or to another app you choose. Websites and other apps you open have their own privacy policies.

  ## Google ML Kit

  Codes are read on your device by Google ML Kit, which is bundled with the app. Your camera images and the content of your
  scans are not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, Android version,
  app and library version, and performance and error metrics, to maintain and improve the library. This is described in
  [Google's ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) and governed by the [Google
  Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

  ## Ratings prompt
  
  After you've used the app for a while, it may show Google Play's in-app rating window. That window is run by Google Play
  under Google's privacy policy. The developer sees only the public rating and review you choose to leave, not who you are.

  ## Advertising

  This version of ScanBee contains no ads and doesn't use your advertising ID. If ads are added in a future version, this
  policy will be updated before that version is released, and you'll be asked for consent where the law requires it.

  ## Data sharing and selling

  The developer doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. If you email the developer, your message and email address
  are used only to reply to you and are not shared.

  ## Security

  Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps can't read. Keeping your device locked and up
  to date helps protect it.

  ## Children
  
  ScanBee isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone,
  including children.

  ## Your choices and rights

  Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on the developer's side. You
  control everything on your device: turn history off, delete it, or uninstall the app. If you have a question about your
  rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact the developer.

  ## Changes
  
  This policy may be updated, for example when ads are introduced. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective
  date.
